If you can't find what you need in this help center, the Argos team is here to help.

## Email support

Email **[support@joinargos.com](mailto:support@joinargos.com)** for help with your account, questions about how Argos works, or to report a problem.

To help us respond quickly, include:

- A description of what you were trying to do
- The matter, workflow, or integration involved, if relevant
- A screenshot or the exact error message, if you saw one
