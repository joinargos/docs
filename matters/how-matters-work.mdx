## What a matter contains

When you open a matter you'll see its sources organized under the **Sources** section. Sources are split into two tabs:

- **Documents** — uploaded files, Drive files, SharePoint files, and other document-type content.
- **Messages** — emails, Slack messages, and other message-type content.

Every source is indexed so you can search it through Ask Argos and reference it in contract reviews.

## Creating a matter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Matters">
    Click **Matters** in the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new matter">

    Click the button to create a new matter. The matter starts with the name **Untitled Matter** — rename it to something meaningful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add at least one source">
    You must add at least one source before Argos can save the matter. Your options:

    - **Add Files** — upload documents from your computer.
    - **Google Drive** — select files from a connected Drive account. <!-- VERIFY: exact button label for the Drive picker on the matter page -->
    - **SharePoint** — select files or folders from a connected SharePoint account. <!-- VERIFY: exact button label for the SharePoint picker on the matter page -->

    <Note>
      A matter created from an Ask Argos conversation cannot also include uploaded or Drive files. These two creation paths are separate.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Argos process">
    Argos syncs the source content, indexes it for search, and populates matter details. You can see progress in the matter view. When processing is complete, the matter is fully searchable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding sources to an existing matter

Open the matter and use **Add Files**, the Drive picker, or the SharePoint picker to attach additional sources. Argos indexes new content automatically.

<Warning>
  When selecting a SharePoint folder, the folder must contain at least one accessible file. An empty or inaccessible folder will cause the import to fail.
</Warning>

## Matter access

Matters are private by default. Access is granted to the creator, users or groups added explicitly, and organization admins. If you land on a matter you cannot view, you'll see **You don't have access to this matter** with a link back to **Back to Matters**.

## Filtering and finding matters

The Matters list supports filters for status (active/closed), matter type, department, priority, owner, and custom fields. Use the search bar to find matters by name or content.
