When you connect an integration, Argos doesn't just make that content searchable — it can also use it to create and update matters without manual effort.

## How automatic filing works

After you connect an integration, Argos monitors incoming content and files relevant items into matters. The exact behavior depends on the integration:

## Filing manually

For any integration, you can always file content into a matter yourself:

1. Open the matter you want to add content to.
2. In the **Sources** section, use the relevant picker or upload control.
3. Argos syncs and indexes the new content.

## What gets filed

Argos attaches source content — not just metadata — to matters. That means the full text of emails, messages, files, and issue descriptions is indexed and searchable through Ask Argos.

<Note>
  Content from connected integrations is scoped to your organization. Other organizations on Argos cannot access your matters or sources.
</Note>
