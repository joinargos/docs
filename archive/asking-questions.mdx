## Opening Ask Argos

Click **Ask Argos** in the left sidebar. The input bar reads *Ask Argos anything about your legal matters...* — type your question and press **Submit**.

## What you can ask

Ask Argos works best for questions that span your archive:

- "Do we have precedent for a non-compete longer than 12 months?"
- "What indemnification language have we agreed to with SaaS vendors?"
- "Show me all NDAs where we accepted mutual termination rights."
- "What happened in the Acme matter regarding data processing?"

Ask Argos searches across matters, files, emails, Slack messages, Linear issues, and other connected sources depending on what you have connected.

## Attaching files to a question

You can attach files directly to a message before submitting. Argos syncs the attached content first, then includes it in the search context for that question.

- **Add Files** — upload a file from your computer. Accepted formats: PDF, TXT, EML, DOCX.
- **Add files from Drive** — select a file from a connected Google Drive account. Accepted formats: PDF, TXT, EML, DOCX, and Google Docs.

<Note>
  If Google Drive isn't connected yet, hovering the Drive button shows: *Connect Google Drive under Settings → Integrations to import Drive files.*
</Note>

## Asking about a specific matter

From any matter page, open the Ask Argos panel on the right. The prompt changes to *Ask Argos about this matter* — answers are scoped to that matter's sources only, which gives you more precise results when you know which matter you're working in.

## Starting a new conversation vs. continuing one

Each question in the same conversation builds on prior context. To start fresh, navigate back to **Ask Argos** from the sidebar — this opens a new conversation.

Previous conversations appear in the sidebar under **Archive** so you can return to them at any time.
