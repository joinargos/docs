The Outlook integration (Microsoft 365) connects your mailbox to Argos. Once connected, Argos syncs your email history and automatically queues relevant threads for matter import.

<Note>
  If your organization restricts user consent for Microsoft applications, an IT administrator must approve the Argos Microsoft 365 application before you connect. See [Microsoft 365 integrations for IT](/it-setup/microsoft-365-integrations).
</Note>

## Connect Outlook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **New Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Outlook">
    Click the **Outlook** tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Microsoft">
    You'll be redirected to Microsoft to authorize Argos. Sign in with the Microsoft account whose mailbox you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the sync status">
    Sync status shows **Sync queued → Sync in progress → Sync completed**. Argos syncs your mailbox folders, messages, threads, and attachments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can connect one Outlook mailbox at a time. To switch mailboxes, go to **Settings → Integrations**, delete the existing Outlook connection, then connect a new one.
</Note>

## Permissions and OAuth scopes

When you authorize Argos, Microsoft asks you to consent to the following delegated Microsoft Graph permissions:

| Consent screen text | Graph scope | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| Maintain access to data you have given it access to | `offline_access` | Lets Argos refresh its access token so syncing continues without you signing in each time. Grants no additional permissions of its own. |
| Sign you in and read your profile | `User.Read` | Signs you in with your organizational account and reads your basic profile and company information. Includes the `openid` and `profile` sign-in scopes. |
| Read your mail | `Mail.Read` | Read-only access to email in your mailbox. |

All three are read or sign-in scopes — Argos cannot send, modify, or delete mail. Access is delegated to the signed-in user; Argos sees only what that user can see.

## What gets synced

The Outlook integration syncs:

- Email threads and individual messages
- Attachments from threads identified as relevant to legal work
- Sync continues automatically — new emails are picked up incrementally after the initial sync

## Automatic matter filing

After syncing, Argos identifies threads that appear relevant to legal work and automatically queues them for matter import. Relevant threads are attached to new or existing matters without manual effort.

## Disconnecting or deleting

To remove the Outlook connection, go to **Settings → Integrations**, find the connection row, and choose **Disconnect** or **Delete**.

- **Disconnect** pauses syncing but preserves indexed data.
- **Delete** removes the connection entirely. The dialog confirms: *This will delete the connection and disconnect it from Microsoft Graph*.
