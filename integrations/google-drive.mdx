Google Drive is a manual-selection integration. You connect your account once, then pick individual files whenever you want to bring them into Argos. Argos does not automatically sync your entire Drive.

## Connect Google Drive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **New Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google Drive">
    Click the **Google Drive** tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Google">
    You'll be redirected to Google to authorize Argos. Sign in and grant the requested access. After authorizing, your Drive connection appears in the integrations list with **Connected** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Drive files in Argos

Once connected, a Drive file picker appears wherever you can add sources in Argos:

- **Ask Argos** — click **Add files from Drive** to attach a file to your question. Argos syncs the file, then includes it in the answer context.
- **Matters** — use the Drive picker in the Sources section to add Drive files to a matter. <!-- VERIFY: exact button label for the Drive picker on the matter creation/edit page -->

### Supported file types

The Drive picker accepts: PDF, TXT, EML (email files), DOCX, and Google Docs. Google Docs are converted automatically. Spreadsheets and presentation files are not supported in the picker.

## What happens after you select a file

1. Argos imports the file and syncs its content.
2. For supported formats, Argos converts and extracts text, running OCR on PDFs where needed.
3. The file is indexed and available for search and citation.

## Disconnecting

To remove the Drive connection, go to **Settings → Integrations**, find the Google Drive row, and choose **Delete**. The dialog confirms: *This will delete the connection and disconnect it from the Google Drive API.*
