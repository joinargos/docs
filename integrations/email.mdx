Argos supports two email integrations — **Gmail** (Google Workspace) and **Outlook** (Microsoft 365). Both work the same way: connect your mailbox, Argos syncs your email history, and relevant threads are automatically queued for matter import.

## Connect Gmail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **New Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Gmail">
    Click the **Gmail** tile (labeled *By Argos*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Google">
    You'll be redirected to Google to authorize Argos. Sign in with the Google account whose email you want to connect and grant the requested access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the sync status">
    After connecting, the connection row shows the sync status: **Sync queued → Sync in progress → Sync completed**. Initial sync may take a few minutes depending on mailbox size.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each Gmail account can only be connected by one Argos user at a time. If you see *This Gmail account is already connected by another user*, contact your Argos administrator.
</Note>

## Connect Outlook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **New Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Outlook">
    Click the **Outlook** tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with Microsoft">
    You'll be redirected to Microsoft to authorize Argos. Sign in with the Microsoft account whose mailbox you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the sync status">
    Sync status shows **Sync queued → Sync in progress → Sync completed**. Argos syncs your mailbox folders, messages, threads, and attachments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can connect one Outlook mailbox at a time. To switch mailboxes, go to **Settings → Integrations**, delete the existing Outlook connection, then connect a new one.
</Note>

## What gets synced

Both Gmail and Outlook sync:

- Email threads and individual messages
- Attachments from threads identified as relevant to legal work
- Sync continues automatically — new emails are picked up incrementally after the initial sync

## Automatic matter filing

After syncing, Argos identifies threads that appear relevant to legal work and automatically queues them for matter import. Relevant threads are attached to new or existing matters without manual effort.

## Disconnecting or deleting

To remove an email connection, go to **Settings → Integrations**, find the connection row, and choose **Disconnect** or **Delete**.

- **Disconnect** pauses syncing but preserves indexed data.
- **Delete** removes the connection entirely. The dialog confirms: *This will delete the connection and disconnect it from the Gmail API* (or Microsoft Graph for Outlook).
