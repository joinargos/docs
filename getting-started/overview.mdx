## How Argos is organized

**Matters** are the central unit. Everything — files, emails, Slack messages, issues — attaches to a matter. The matter gives you a single place to see all activity, run contract reviews, and ask questions scoped to that work.

**The Archive** is your organization-wide memory. Ask Argos searches across all matters and connected sources at once, citing exactly where each piece of the answer came from.

**Workflows** define how contract reviews work. You configure the terms you care about once per contract type; Argos applies them every time you run a review.

**Integrations** keep everything in sync. Connect the tools your team already uses and Argos keeps the relevant content indexed automatically.
