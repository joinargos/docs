---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Get from zero to your first matter in a few minutes."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an integration">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **New Connection**. Choose Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Google Drive, SharePoint, Linear, or Asana. Following the on-screen prompts connects your account and starts syncing relevant content into Argos.

    You can use Argos without any integrations, but connecting at least one lets you file matters directly from your existing tools.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first matter">
    {/* VERIFY: exact button label for creating a new matter from the Matters list */}
    Click **Matters** in the left navigation, then click the button to create a new matter. Give it a name and add at least one source — upload a file, or pick one from Google Drive or SharePoint.

    Argos indexes the source content automatically. When processing is complete the matter is ready to search and review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Click **Ask Argos** in the left navigation. Type a question — for example, "Do we have any NDA precedents with a three-year term?" — and press **Submit**. Argos searches your archive and returns a cited answer with links back to the original sources.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run a contract review (optional)">
    If your team reviews contracts, open **Workflows**, click **Add Workflow**, and configure the terms you want evaluated. Then upload a contract to run your first review. See [Contract Review](/contract-review/create-workflow) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Processing time after adding sources depends on file size and type. Most uploads are ready within a minute or two.
</Note>
