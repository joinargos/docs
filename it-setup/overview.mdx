This section is for identity, security, and collaboration administrators preparing Argos for their organization.

<Warning>
  Argos login and Microsoft 365 integrations use separate applications. Configuring SSO does not approve Outlook or SharePoint access, and approving Outlook or SharePoint does not configure SSO.
</Warning>

## What your organization may configure

| Capability | Configured through | Customer IT action |
|---|---|---|
| Single sign-on | WorkOS and your identity provider | Register an application, configure the connection, and assign users or groups. |
| User provisioning | WorkOS Directory Sync and your identity provider | Configure SCIM, assign users or groups, and enable provisioning. |
| Outlook and SharePoint | Argos's multitenant Microsoft application | Grant administrator consent once, then let individual users connect their accounts in Argos. |
| Other integrations | The integration provider | Follow the provider authorization flow described on the relevant integration page. |

## What Argos provides

Depending on the features your organization enables, Argos provides:

- A secure, organization-specific WorkOS setup link for SSO and user provisioning
- The redirect URI for your SSO application registration
- The SCIM endpoint and bearer token when user provisioning is enabled
- The fixed Argos Microsoft application client ID for Outlook and SharePoint
- Support during configuration and testing

<Note>
  Setup links, redirect URIs, SCIM endpoints, bearer tokens, and tenant-specific consent links can be unique to your organization. Do not copy values from another organization's setup or publish secret values in tickets or documentation.
</Note>

## Before rollout

- Identify the administrator responsible for identity and application consent.
- Decide which users or groups should have access to Argos.
- Confirm whether you need SSO only or SSO with SCIM provisioning.
- Confirm whether users need Outlook, SharePoint, or both.
- Plan ownership and rotation for any client secret or bearer token created during setup.
- Test with a small user group before assigning Argos broadly.

If you need organization-specific setup values, email **[support@joinargos.com](mailto:support@joinargos.com)**.
