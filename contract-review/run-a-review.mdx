## Start a review from a workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workflow">
    Click **Workflows** in the left navigation and open the workflow you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the contract">
    <!-- VERIFY: exact button label and upload mechanism to start a review from the workflow detail page -->
    Use the upload control on the workflow page to attach the contract file. Accepted formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT, EML, XLSX, XLS, XML, MP4, MP3, MOV.

    <Note>
      Not all accepted file types produce a full AI analysis. For best results, use PDF or DOCX. If you have questions about a specific format, check with your Argos administrator.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for analysis">
    Argos creates a matter for the review, then evaluates each configured term in parallel. The review page shows progress as terms complete. Most reviews finish within a few minutes depending on contract length and number of terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Argos evaluates

For each term, Argos:

1. Reads the contract for relevant language.
2. Extracts the answer to the configured question.
3. Applies your rules to assign a rating (Supported, Attention, Needs Further Review, or Blocked).
4. Cites the specific contract language that supports its assessment.

**Template comparison** — if your workflow term includes **Template language**, Argos also compares the contract's language to your standard clause and flags material differences.

**Paper classification** — when a template is configured on the workflow, Argos classifies the contract as **Self-advised Paper** (your paper) or **Third-Party Paper** (counterparty's paper) and runs an additional check for material outside-template deviations not covered by your configured terms.

## Uploading a new contract version

You can upload a revised contract version once the current review is complete. While a review is still in progress, the upload control shows: *Finish the current contract review before uploading a new version.* Once complete, upload the new version and Argos creates a new review linked to the same matter. Previous versions remain accessible via the **Version** selector at the top of the review page.
